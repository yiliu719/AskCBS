## Recruiting Playbooks by Industry/Function

### Investment Banking
- **On-campus recruiting (OCR) 2026–2027 timeline:**
  - Firmwide Presentations: Sept 21, 2026 – mid-October
  - Informational Interviews (coffee chats): early October – early December
  - Invitation-Only Events: early November – mid-December
  - Cover Letter/Resume Drops: early December (dates vary)
  - First Round Interviews: begin Jan 4, 2027
  - Superday (final round) Interviews: begin Jan 7, 2027
  - Offers can be extended starting 5PM ET, Fri Jan 11, 2027
  - Post-OCR recruiting continues informally through spring
- Business-formal attire expected almost every weekday late Sept–December.
- Key advice: jump in early — banks whittle down candidate lists fast via firmwides/coffee chats;
  if you have *any* interest, join IBC and start the process even if unsure.
- Recommended clubs: Investment Banking Club, Restructuring & Distressed Investing Group, plus
  any social/affinity club (bankers like hearing about your life outside recruiting) and any
  industry-specific club matching your target coverage group.
- CMC Advisor: Anjna Motwani. Faculty with IB experience: Donna Hitscherich, Brian Lancaster.

### Management Consulting
- **Timeline:** Aug–Sept resume/networking prep → Sept–Oct firm-wide networking events/coffee
  chats → Oct–Dec continued casing prep + firmwide events → Nov–Dec application submissions →
  January interviews.
- MCA (Management Consulting Association) has 250+ members; runs case coaching cohorts,
  resource library, mentorship.
- Resources: Case in Point, Case Interview Secrets, RocketBlocks-style drilling tools.
- Key CMC contacts: Joni Coughlin (Sr. Associate Director, Consulting), Elana Sable (Sr.
  Associate Director, Employer Relations, Consulting).

### Private Equity
- Members typically fall into 3 buckets: (1) committed to PE internships, (2) pursuing PE-
  adjacent work (diligence consulting, value creation, sponsors IB), (3) generally interested,
  possibly as a 2nd/3rd career move.
- **Timeline:** Megafunds (KKR, Blackstone, Apollo, etc.) — Oct to Jan. Other funds (mid-market,
  lower middle market) — Jan to May.
- PE Club runs a modeling/case study training program in the first semester; KKR case
  competition recommended especially without prior PE/IB experience.
- A **Private Equity Program** exists for 2nd-years (apply end of first year) — alumni
  mentorship, preferred course access, guest speakers, modeling tests, interview prep.
- Many club members don't land direct PE roles but land adjacent ones (secondaries, growth
  equity, M&A at startups, sponsors IB) — landing anything in the space takes independently
  driven effort; many offers don't land until April/May.
- Key CMC/Faculty: Laurie Boockvar, Jon Hicks (CMC); Greta Larson (PE Program); Michael Ewens,
  Donna Hitscherich (PE courses/curriculum).

### Venture Capital
- VC recruiting is "just-in-time" — firms look for interns 1–3 months before start date. No
  fixed timeline like IB/consulting.
- **Suggested cadence:**
  - First semester: talk to 2nd-years in VC, network with firms, look for in-semester
    opportunities.
  - Winter break: develop an investment thesis/AI project; build a target firm list matching
    your thesis.
  - Early spring: reach out to target firms, socialize your thesis; consider an in-semester
    internship.
  - Late spring: focus recruiting push, apply broadly, lean on connections made all year.
- Resources: Pitchbook, CBInsights, the Columbia Venture Fellows program (apply if VC-focused).
- Key CMC/Faculty: Irene Papajohn, Sunita Lokuge (CMC); Angela Lee, Jorge Guzman (Faculty).
- Recommended clubs: VC Club + an industry-specific club matching your investing thesis (RLG,
  Tech Club, AI Club, Healthcare, etc.)

### Technology (Product/Strategy/PM roles)
- **MBA Tech Recruiting Timeline** (structured programs, e.g. APM/rotational):
  - Aug–Sept: Resume prep (peak activity Sept)
  - Sept–Apr: Networking (ongoing, high activity through fall)
  - Sept–Nov: Applications & resume drops (peak Oct–Nov)
  - Nov–Mar: Interview season (peak Dec–Feb)
  - Sept–Mar: Interview prep (peak Nov–Jan)
  - Aug–Apr: In-semester opportunities (ongoing)
  - *Big Tech and later-stage startups may start even earlier (Sept/Oct).*
- Advice: apply broadly including tech roles at non-tech companies (financial services, retail,
  consumer goods, healthcare all have strong product/strategy roles); AI fluency (Cursor, Notion
  AI, Perplexity) increasingly a baseline PM expectation; get comfortable with Python/SQL basics
  for technical PM roles.
- Suggested pre-MBA reading: Stratechery, Lenny's Newsletter, The Pragmatic Engineer, Not Boring
  (blogs); Acquired, Decoder (The Verge), Lenny's Podcast, Land of the Giants (podcasts).
- Recommended clubs: Tech Club, AI Club, CEO (Columbia Entrepreneurs Organization), Fintech &
  Blockchain, VC Club, Alleycon.

### Artificial Intelligence (AI-specific roles)
- Two tracks, not mutually exclusive:
  - **Structured** (Big Tech AI/PM/strategy): resume drops open as early as August; networking
    and applications ramp Sept–Nov; interviews run late fall into early spring. Treat like IB/
    consulting timelines.
  - **Unstructured** (labs, startups, AI-focused VC): mostly just-in-time, concentrated in
    spring, networking-driven.
- Interview prep leans on product sense + AI-specific judgment questions (e.g., "how would you
  add AI to this product, and what are the risks?"). Use Exponent for mocks.
- AI Club runs hands-on workshops (prompting, APIs, agentic tools), hackathons with OpenAI/
  Google/Anthropic, an annual West Coast trek (past: Google, Atlassian, NVIDIA, Apple, Adobe).

### Real Estate
- Most opportunities occur Q4 of first year into Q2/spring. Larger companies begin recruiting
  early Q4.
- Key events: REA/CMC/PMC panels in early fall, Career Forum in February, Women in Real Estate
  Conference, REA coffee chats year-round, company recruiting on-campus Nov/Dec.
- Key contacts: Gabe Tishman & Ben Curry (REA Co-Presidents), Leanne Lachman & Kristen Svennigsen
  & Alexa Hammel (PMC), Brian Lancaster (Faculty).

### Healthcare
- **Timeline:** Pharma industry events (on-campus + coffee chats) begin fall; first-round
  interviews within first two weeks of January; second/final rounds complete by mid-February.
  Consulting/banking healthcare roles follow those clubs' timelines. Digital health and PE/VC
  healthcare roles are enterprise/just-in-time, typically spring.
- Annual HCIA Healthcare Conference: Feb 5, 2027.
- HCIA = student-led; HPM (Healthcare and Pharmaceutical Management) Program = faculty-led
  curriculum side; they partner on programming.
- Key contacts: Long Wu, Diane Matuzak (CMC); Carrie Chan (HPM faculty director); Cliff Cramer
  (HPM faculty director emeritus).

### Marketing
- Recruiting begins fall, continues year-round — some companies/industries structured (Oct
  start), others just-in-time (spring).
- Key CMC contact: Irene Papajohn.
- Recommended clubs: Marketing Association (MAC), Retail & Luxury Goods Club, General Management
  Association.

### General Management
- No strict recruiting timeline — begins in September, cycle can run as late as May.
- Broadest MBA career path: Leadership Development Programs (LDPs), corporate strategy,
  operations, product management, startup leadership.
- GMA pairs well as a complement to industry-specific clubs.
- Key CMC contact: Pamela Harvey.

### Social Enterprise / Impact
- Most recruiting opportunities are in spring; some fellowship applications (e.g. Tamer
  Institute's ReAP program) happen earlier.
- Works closely with the Tamer Institute for Social Enterprise and Climate Change.
- Key contacts: Michael De Lucia (CMC); Ivy Hatsengate, Sandra Navalli, Bruce Usher (Tamer
  Institute).
- Recommended clubs: Social Enterprise Club, Leo Impact Fund (formerly Microlumbia), Pangea
  Advisors, Non-Profit Board Leadership Program. Depending on interest: Green Business Club,
  Columbia Entrepreneurs Organization (CEO).

### Sustainability / Green Business (Climate/ESG-adjacent)
- **Timeline:** Traditional paths (consulting, finance, general management) — fall term.
  Non-traditional (startups, VC) — spring term.
- Recommended: Green Business Club (GBC), industry-interest clubs (Energy & Infrastructure,
  Retail & Luxury, VC Club), Social Enterprise Club, Pangea Advisors, LEO Impact Fund. Three
  Cairns Fellowship (not a club) applications due late September.
- Key CMC/Faculty: Laurie Boockvar (CMC), Ivy Hatsengate (Tamer Center).
- Advice: attend Climate Week NYC events in September to help narrow focus areas; consider
  cross-registering in SUMA, SIPA, or Columbia Climate School courses.

### Energy & Infrastructure
- Career pathways: Finance & Investing (IB, PE/infrastructure funds, VC, climate tech investing,
  project finance, asset management), Corporate & Strategy roles, Startups & Entrepreneurship
  (EV infrastructure, distributed energy, grid software), Commodity Trading, Policy & Nonprofit.
- **Timeline:** Traditional (IB, consulting, corporate strategy) — fall term, mostly on-campus,
  structured. Non-traditional (startups, VC) — spring term, sometimes earlier, more
  networking-driven.
- Key CMC contacts: Laurie Boockvar, Nicholas Kalogeropoulos.
- Recommended reading: Columbia Energy Exchange, Axios Future of Energy, Canary Media, Latitude
  Media, Utility Dive.
- Recommended clubs to pair with: Investment Banking Club, Private Equity Club, Venture Capital
  Club, Management Consulting Club, Columbia Entrepreneurs Organization, Quantitative Markets and
  Trading Club, Social Enterprise Club, Green Business Club.

### Entrepreneurship Through Acquisition (Search Funds)
- CETA is the primary career development group for ETA/search fund recruiting (many
  opportunities are offered to CETA before CMC).
- No formalized recruiting timeline; internship opportunities have been trending earlier each
  year. Internships fall into three phases: searching, diligence, operating — try to get exposure
  across all three.
- Formal summer internship programs exist via Pacific Lake, Relay Investments, Anacapa Partners.
- Recommended reading: HBR Guide to Buying a Small Business, Buy Then Build (Walker Diebel),
  Stanford's Search Fund Primer, Acquiring Minds podcast.
- Key CMC/Faculty: Laurie Boockvar, Jon Hicks (CMC — same as PE); Clayton Sachs, Tom Jackson,
  Will Bressman, King GJ, Carlos Gilla (Faculty).
- Contact: infocbseta.com

### Fintech & Blockchain
- Recruiting generally in line with tech recruiting timing; begins fall, continues all year.
- Key CMC/Faculty: Sunita Lokuge (CMC); Stephen Zeldes, Omid Malekan (Faculty).
- Recommended clubs: Tech Club, VC Club, General Management, AI Club, Alleycon, Gaming &
  Esports Club.

### Retail & Luxury Goods
- Recruiting is majority just-in-time; recruiting timelines are often shorter than other
  industries, so build relationships early.
- Key CMC contacts: Joni Coughlin (CMC), Amanda Sanches (RLG CMC Liaison).
- Recommended reading: WWD, Business of Fashion, Consumer Reports.

### Beauty
- Recruiting is largely unstructured and relationship-driven; no universal timeline. Companies
  range from legacy multinationals (L'Oréal, Estée Lauder, LVMH, Shiseido) to indie brands and
  beauty-focused investors.
- Annual Beauty Conference (4th edition featured L'Oréal, e.l.f. Beauty leadership); Instagram:
  @ecolumbiabeautyclub.
- Recommended pre-MBA reading: CEW, Glossy, WWD, Beauty Independent, Beauty Matters, Business of
  Fashion, Vogue Business.

### Media
- Media roles are largely "unstructured" recruiting — roles pop up year-round, mostly spring,
  self-driven.
- **Timeline:** Sept–Oct industry research/opinion-forming → Oct–Nov targeted LinkedIn outreach
  + interview prep → Dec prep for career treks (incl. West Coast Trek to LA) → Jan–Apr key
  recruiting window (don't rely solely on CBS job postings — set Google Alerts/LinkedIn searches)
  → Apr–May expand search beyond media if needed.
- Key CMC/Faculty: Diane Matuzak (CMC & Employer Relations), Jennifer Merchant (Career
  Advisor/Coach).
- Recommended reading: The Hollywood Reporter, Deadline, Variety, AdWeek, The Town.

### Hospitality & Travel
- Recruiting runs November through May; not on an established timeline like consulting/banking/
  tech — reach out to contacts early regardless.
- Recommended clubs: Gourmet Club, Wine Society, Real Estate Association.

### Sports Business
- Sports recruiting is largely driven by individual companies/personal relationships rather than
  formal on-campus processes; the industry is small and competitive.
- Major professional league internship programs fill most positions Jan–March; applications may
  close 1–2 months prior to stated deadlines — apply early.
- Key CMC/Faculty: Mike De Lucia, Jon Hicks, Hollis O'Rorke, Jonathan Knee.
- Recommended clubs: Sports Business Association, Media Management Association.

### Trading & Markets (Quant/Front-Office)
- Covers Sales & Trading, Quant Trading/Research, Risk Management, Portfolio Management,
  Sales/Client-facing roles, Compliance/Operations, Financial Consulting, Data Science, Product
  across banks, asset managers, trading firms (HFT + quant hedge funds), and TradFi/crypto
  exchanges.
- **Timeline:** Two waves. First wave (large established hedge funds, certain banks) begins
  September through New Year's. Second wave (large asset managers, certain banks, MBA/MS
  programs) begins November into February. Some hiring happens year-round/opportunistically.
- QMT resources: resume book sent to partner companies/headhunters, job board, interview
  question bank, workshops (technical/non-technical).
- Key CMC/Faculty contacts: Vyju Manian, Ryan Barlow (CMC); Professors Harry Mamaysky, Michael
  Johannes, Achilles Venetoulias, Austin Campbell, Omid Malekan; Melina Denebeim (Senior
  Director MSFE).

### Credit (IB, Investing, Private Credit / Restructuring)
- No prior finance experience required for IB-track roles; helps but isn't mandatory for credit
  roles.
- **Timeline:** Restructuring roles — identical to IB timeline (mid-Sept–early Jan). Credit
  roles — identical to IM/PE for on-campus (late Sept–early Feb); Dec–May for off-campus.
- Only independent advisors (non-bulge-bracket) recruit dedicated restructuring (RX) analysts —
  8–10ish banks run processes; RX can be recruited in parallel with M&A/coverage at most firms.
- Recommended resources: restructuringinterviews.com, petition11, Wall Street Prep Red Book
  (restructuring section), BIWS Guides, The Credit Investor's Handbook (Michael Gatto).
- Contacts: Marina Arantes (MArantes27@gsb.columbia.edu), Anmol Sakarda
  (ASakarda27@gsb.columbia.edu), Nic Smoller (NSmoller27@gsb.columbia.edu).

### Aerospace & Defense Tech
- Recruiting is typically ad-hoc/just-in-time; ADT can function as a standalone professional club
  or as an interest overlay for IB/PE/VC/Consulting with an A&D focus.
- Open to civilians and veterans alike; collaborates with SIPA, the Engineering School, and local
  industry partners.
- Recommended pre-MBA reading: War on the Rocks, Defense One, Lawfare; podcasts Solarpunk,
  Defense One Radio, Net Assessment.
- Key CMC contact: Jon Hicks.
- Recommended clubs: Veterans Club, Tech Club, VC Club, Investment Banking Club.

## Notes on Interpreting This Section

- Nearly every club emphasizes the same core advice: **start early, build genuine relationships
  (not transactional asks), and don't wait for a "formal" process if the industry doesn't have
  one.**
- "Structured" recruiting (IB, Consulting, some Tech/Trading tracks) runs almost entirely in the
  fall semester with hard deadlines. "Unstructured"/"enterprise" recruiting (VC, PE, Media,
  Beauty, Hospitality, Real Estate, Social Enterprise, Sustainability-adjacent roles) is
  networking-driven and can extend well into spring — sometimes not resolving until April/May.
- Many clubs recommend joining 1–2 complementary clubs beyond your primary interest (e.g., a
  functional club like IB/Consulting/VC *plus* an industry-vertical club like Healthcare, Retail,
  Energy, etc.) to build a credible, specific story.
