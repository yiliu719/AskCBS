## Campus Overview

In Spring 2022, Columbia Business School moved into the Manhattanville campus. It consists of
two buildings facing each other across a green quad:

- **Henry R. Kravis Hall** ("Kravis") — closer to the Hudson River
- **David Geffen Hall** ("Geffen") — closer to Broadway

The campus is also home to the Lenfest Center for the Arts, the Forum, and the Jerome L. Greene
Science Center.

### Kravis Hall
- Odd floors are faculty offices
- 1st Floor Steps: overlooks the quad between Kravis and Geffen
- 2nd floor: **Smith Dining Hall** — the largest on-campus food selection
- 4th floor: student floor — screening room, game room, lounge, quiet study room

**Microwaves in Kravis:**
- Smith Dining Hall (2nd floor) — 2 microwaves
- 4th Floor Game Room — 1 microwave
- Basement, beside the lockers — 1 microwave

### Geffen Hall
- 1st floor: Joe's Cafe (open to the public)
- 1st floor: Cooperman Commons — the biggest lecture hall
- 2nd floor: I-Lab and group study space
- 3rd floor: Horton Lounge

**Microwaves in Geffen:**
- 1st Floor — 1 microwave
- 3rd Floor — 1 microwave
- 6th Floor — 2 microwaves

### The Link
Tunnel connecting Kravis and Geffen. Hosts student lockers and the "gym."

### Near-Campus Workout Spots
ClassPass, Crunch Gym, Dodge Gym (Columbia University Gym, 3030 Broadway — free for enrolled
full-time MBA students with ID; waiver required first time; extra charges for lockers, fitness
classes, personal training, club sports), Equinox, Method Collective, Health & Wellness Club
(discounted classes with peers).

### Near-Campus Dining Spots
Dear Mama (great coffee), Pisticci, Red Olive Deli, Columbia Deli and Cafe, Jin Ramen, Dumpling
House, Dino BBQ, Maki.

### Near-Campus Bar Spots
Amity Hall, Arts & Crafts Beer Parlor, Baylander, Lola La Marina, The Craftsman, The Expat.

**Pro-tip:** Check the #scavengers Slack channel — students post free leftovers from campus
events.

## CBS Hardware (given during Orientation)

- **Casebook (iPad):** Locked CBS-issued iPad + Apple Pencil, set up during Orientation. Used to
  read cases and take notes in class. Cannot access email, social media, or other restricted
  sites — school resources only (Canvas, course materials, PollEverywhere, etc.). Kept after
  graduation and unlocked for unrestricted use. IT help: Geffen Hall, Room 460.
- **Name Plate:** Plastic, personalized, fits into the groove on classroom desks in Manhattanville.
  Bring daily — professors use it to learn names, verify attendance, and give participation credit.
- **Name Tag:** Smaller magnetic tag for on-campus events/recruiting sessions. Updating your name
  plate does NOT automatically update your name tag.
- **Student ID:** Your building access ticket. If forgotten, front desk at either building can
  grant one-time access after confirming enrollment. Lost ID replacement: $20 at the Manhattanville
  Office of Public Safety, 3270 Broadway (across the street from campus).

## CBS Systems ("at least 5 systems for anything")

- **UNI:** Initials + 4 numbers (e.g., JD1234) — Columbia-wide identifier.
- **Lionmail** (uni@columbia.edu / JD1234@columbia.edu): Lifetime Google account. Some Google
  docs/sheets are only accessible signed into this account.
- **Outlook email** (First initial + last name + grad year @gsb.columbia.edu, e.g.,
  JDoe26@gsb.columbia.edu): **Use this one for internships and alumni outreach.**
- **UNI email** (uni@gsb.columbia.edu): Only used for signing into Outlook; goes to the same place
  as the Outlook inbox.
- **Duo:** Required multi-factor auth for all CBS systems — download the app, enable push
  notifications.
- **12twenty:** Career Management Center (CMC) system — job postings, resume, booking CMC fellow
  time.
- **Casebook:** The locked CBS iPad (see Hardware above).
- **PawPrint:** Campus printing system. Pro-tip: set up and test-print well before your LEAD exam.
- **Canvas (Courseworks):** Class portal — syllabus, files, assignments, exams.
- **SSOL/Vergil:** Registration, financial services (award info, loan history, tuition, account
  details), academic records (transcripts, degree status).
- **CampusGroups:** Connects to all student orgs/events. Also used to reserve study rooms.
  Pro-tip: download the CampusGroups app.
- **Groupchats (Slack):** Primary official comms channel for most clubs/orgs/clusters. Pro-tip:
  turn on notifications only for a couple of professional clubs you need real-time; check others
  once a day.
- **Starfish:** Scheduling appointments with your academic advisor or a peer tutor.

## Orientation & First Weeks

- Orientation is your first week on campus. You'll learn your Cluster assignment (~70–75 people
  per Cluster) at check-in.
- Expect Cluster-specific meetings led by Peer Advisors (PAs), large-group sessions, and — toward
  the end of the week — a few actual core classes to ease into the academic workload.
- **Dress code:** Smart casual with comfortable footwear for Orientation week; CBS dress code
  overall ranges from t-shirts/shorts to office wear depending on the day.
- **Peer Advisors (PAs):** 3–4 assigned per Cluster; your first go-to resource for any CBS
  question. Overseen by a 2026–2027 PA Board (PA Chair: Anna Glickman).

## CBS Systems Note — "The Core"

CBS has 4 types of classes, worth 1.5 or 3 credit hours:
- **Block Week** (Half or Full): condensed into 3 days–1 week, taken before the regular semester
  starts.
- **Full Term:** entire 12-week semester.
- **A-Term:** first 6 weeks of the semester.
- **B-Term:** final 6 weeks of the semester.

**First semester core setup:**
| Block Week | Full Term |
|---|---|
| Lead: People, Teams, and Organizations | Financial Accounting |

| A-Term | B-Term |
|---|---|
| Foundations of Valuation | Corporate Finance |
| Managerial Statistics | Business Analytics |
| Strategy Formulation | Marketing |
| Managerial Economics | Global Economic Environment |

One additional core class, **Operations Management**, is taken in the A-Term of second semester.

## Learning Teams & Cluster Boards

- **Learning Teams:** Smaller subset of your Cluster (4–6 people), assigned during Orientation.
  Serves as your project team for all core classes. You'll draft a Learning Team Charter early on
  (meeting cadence, roles, responsibilities).
- **Cluster Board Elections:** After the first few weeks, your Cluster elects: Cluster Chair,
  Academic Rep, Career Rep, Global Citizenship Rep, Chief of Staff, Inclusive Engagement Rep,
  Community Rep, Events Rep (1 each).

## CBS Traditions & Culture

- **Grade Non-Disclosure:** Student norm (voted annually, ~95% support) — students do not
  disclose grades/GPA/transcripts to employers until accepting a full-time offer. Academic
  honors and honor society membership CAN still be disclosed.
- **CBS Matters:** Long-standing tradition where students share their personal story; organized
  per Cluster, open to friends from other Clusters.
- **Cluster Cup:** Spring inter-Cluster competition — trivia, scavenger hunts, soccer, a fun run,
  etc. — for glory and cluster funds.
- **Cluster Auction:** Part of Tamer Center's Social Impact Week; students auction off skills,
  events, or personality items to clustermates.
- **Follies:** Once-a-semester show of student-written sketches/dances parodying CBS life. Has
  three "arms": Follies (skits/videos), Juranimal (CBS's cover band), Follies Dance.
- **Fall & Spring Galas:** Black-tie ExBo-hosted events with photo booths, live bands, DJs.
- **Rugby Networking Social:** Weekly open-bar social hosted by CBS RFC (Rugby Football Club),
  co-sponsored with rotating clubs at a local bar. Open to all.
- **Club Day:** Early fall semester — meet 2nd-years about their club experiences before an open
  enrollment period. CBS has 80+ student-led clubs.
- **AVP Program:** First-years can apply to be an Assistant Vice President for various clubs —
  a good way to build experience and meet 2nd-years.

## Recruiting 101 (CMC Overview)

- **CMC Advisors:** 9 advisors — professional goals, recruiting paths, timing/prioritization,
  interview/logistics help, personal branding.
- **Coaches:** 40+ CBS alumni across sectors giving real-time industry/marketplace perspective.
- **CMC Fellows:** 2nd-years who've been through recruiting — especially helpful in the first
  month.
- **Executives-in-Residence:** 20+ current/former C-suite execs available for career-vision
  conversations.
- **Recruiting & Networking Events:** Hundreds/year hosted by CMC with employers.
- **Class of 2023 stats:** 89% received a job offer within 3 months of graduation; median base
  salary $175,000.
- Pro-tip: you'll be added to a dedicated Slack channel for CMC/ExBo recruiting updates — turn
  on notifications.

## Transportation

### Columbia University Evening Shuttle (Manhattanville ↔ Morningside / campus area)
Free, on-demand transportation service run by Columbia Transportation in partnership with
Public Safety and **Via**.
- **How to book:** Download the "Columbia University Evening Shuttle" app (Apple App Store /
  Google Play), log in with your Columbia UNI and password, create an account (name must match
  your Columbia ID, plus Columbia email and phone number).
- **No app?** Call **646-692-0576** for a ride.
- **Coverage zone:** Roughly 103rd St (south) to 137th St (north), Broadway (west) to St.
  Nicholas Ave / Frederick Douglass Blvd (east). Exact boundaries vary slightly by avenue.
- This is the go-to option for getting between the Manhattanville campus and the Morningside
  Heights campus (or elsewhere within the zone) at night as an alternative to walking alone.

### Subway
- CBS's Manhattanville campus is at W. 130th St & Broadway.
- Closest subway stop: **125th Street (1 train)** — 5-minute walk from CBS.
- Alternative: A, C, B, or D train to 125th St & St. Nicholas Ave — ~15-minute walk to CBS.
- Columbia's Morningside Heights ("main") campus closest stop: **116th Street** ("116 St –
  Columbia University"), served by the 1 train only.
- Recommended apps: myMTA, or Google Maps.

**Key subway stops:**
| Stop | Description | Line(s) |

| 125th Street | CBS, Manhattanville Campus | 1 |
| 116th Street | Columbia University, Morningside Campus | 1 |
| 96th Street | Good for connecting 2/3 (express) ↔ 1 (local) | 1, 2, 3 |

### Gym & Sports
**Columbia Dodge Fitness Center**, 3030 Broadway (northwest side of Morningside campus). Most
equipment/facilities free for enrolled full-time MBA students with student ID; waiver required

### Health & Health Insurance
- **Columbia Health**, 4th floor of John Jay Hall (511 W 114th St), on the Morningside campus
  near Butler Library. Book via the health portal (secure.health.columbia.edu), call
  212-854-7426, or email healthecolumbia.edu.
- All full-time students must have adequate health insurance (Jan 1 – Aug 14 coverage window);
  waiver available if your own plan meets University criteria — see the Columbia Health website.

### Schooling / Academics
- **Exemption Exams:** Test out of required core classes with sufficient prior experience.
  Questions: exemptions@gsb.columbia.edu
- **Pre-MBA Work:** Canvas has a Pre-MBA section with accounting/quant/Excel prep resources.
- **Tutoring:** Full-time MBA/EMBA students get unlimited free peer tutoring for core courses +
  Capital Markets and Investments. Book via Starfish. Additional support: Office of Student
  Affairs (Brandon Manning, Director, Advising and Academic Success). English Language Tutoring:
  5 hrs/semester (20 hrs total), email tutor@gsb.columbia.edu.
- **Career Management Center (CMC):** 7th floor, Geffen Hall.
- **Financial Aid:** Geffen 280.
- **Jerome A. Chazen Institute for Global Business:** interdisciplinary hub for global business
  knowledge at CBS.

### Banking/Immigration for International Students
- **ISSO (International Students and Scholars Office):** All F-1/J-1 visa holders must report
  arrival within one week via isso.columbia.edu/content/reporting-your-arrival-students. ISSO
  maintains immigration status compliance throughout your time at CBS. More info: isso.columbia.edu
